Pressure Equipment & Systems

Process Equipment

Pressure vessels, process tanks, heat exchangers, deaerators, water softening units, flue gas cleaning systems and auxiliary equipment.

Pressure Vessels

Designed and manufactured to operate safely under high pressure, our pressure vessels comply with international codes and standards and serve the energy, chemical, food and many other industries with proven reliability.

  • CE, EAC and Gospromnazor certified manufacturing
  • Design to AD 2000 and EN 13445 standards
  • Steam accumulators, air receivers, expansion tanks
  • Long service life and safe operation
  • Custom design options

Process Tanks

Custom-engineered to the needs of your production process, our process tanks are built in a range of volumes and configurations for mixing, storage, conditioning and process safety.

  • Hard and soft candy cooking units
  • Sesame tahini production tanks
  • Turkish delight production units
  • Winterization and batch-type extraction
  • Neutralization and bleaching units
  • Custom design for specific process needs

Heat Exchangers

Delivering highly efficient heat transfer between different fluids, our heat exchangers provide ideal solutions for energy savings and process efficiency.

  • High-efficiency heat transfer
  • Corrosion-resistant materials
  • Compact, modular construction
  • Compliant with ASME and EN standards
  • Energy savings and process efficiency

Deaerators

In steam systems, our deaerators remove oxygen and other dissolved gases from the feedwater to prevent corrosion, extending the service life of your boilers and piping.

  • Removal of oxygen and dissolved gases
  • Full compatibility with steam boilers
  • Automatic control systems
  • Corrosion prevention and longer boiler life
  • Low operating costs

Water Softening Units

By removing hardness from the water in industrial steam and hot water systems, our softening units prevent scale formation, reduce energy losses and protect your equipment.

  • Hardness removal technology
  • Automatic regeneration
  • Suitable for steam and hot water systems
  • PLC-controlled operation
  • Scale prevention and extended system life

Flue Gas Cleaning Systems

By filtering particulates and harmful components from post-combustion flue gas, our systems ensure environmentally responsible, low-emission operation in full compliance with standards.

  • Cyclones and multicyclones
  • Bag filters
  • Dry-type ESP electrostatic precipitators
  • Flue gas scrubbing units
  • Full compliance with environmental standards

Auxiliary Equipment

Fuel feeding, conveying, storage and waste handling systems that keep power plants running efficiently and without interruption.

  • Fuel feeding systems (moving floor, screw, hydraulic pusher)
  • Belt conveyors and chain conveyors
  • Pneumatic dust feeding lines
  • Ash discharge and handling systems (wet/dry)
  • Fuel storage and dosing systems
  • Silo and bunker systems

Our expert engineering team is ready to deliver the solutions best suited to your needs.
