Energy Generation Systems

Complete boiler and heat generation systems for your industrial energy needs

BRV / BRV-RK Series

Thermal Oil Boilers

Bersey supplies complete thermal oil boilers and waste heat recovery systems fired by gaseous, liquid or solid fuels. Thermal oil systems are a reliable way to deliver continuous heat at high temperatures while operating at low system pressures.

  • BRV model: double-helical coil design, 465 kW – 23 MW capacity range
  • BRV-RK model: 12 MW – 46 MW capacity, radiant and convective heat transfer
  • Efficiency of up to 90% (LHV)
  • Low film temperature thanks to the double-helical coil design
  • Safe operating temperatures up to 350°C

Scotch Type

Steam Boilers

Complete steam boilers and waste heat recovery systems fired by gaseous, liquid or solid fuels

BRS Type

3-Pass Scotch Steam Boilers

BRS-type steam boilers are manufactured in capacities from 1,000 to 20,000 kg/h. They are burner-fired, fire-tube boilers with a cylindrical shell. Thanks to the 3-pass design, flue gases stay inside the boiler longer, delivering high heat transfer.

  • Cylindrical shell built to withstand high pressure
  • Fully cylindrical furnace for homogeneous combustion
  • Large heat transfer surface provided by fire tubes
  • 3-pass design with low flue gas temperature
  • Economizer option for increased system efficiency
Membrane Water Wall

Combined Steam Boilers

A solution that combines a membrane water-wall combustion chamber with a single-pass flame and fire-tube boiler. The combustion chamber is enclosed by water-cooled membrane walls.

  • Safe operation with membrane water walls
  • Single-pass flue gas flow, compact design
  • High thermal efficiency, low operating cost
  • Durable combustion chamber with minimal refractory requirement
Water Tube

Water-Tube Steam Boilers

Membrane-wall water-tube steam boilers are modern, reliable energy generation systems designed for industrial applications that require high pressure and capacity.

  • Membrane water-wall combustion chamber
  • Water-tube design for high pressure and capacity
  • Fast steam generation and high efficiency
  • Minimal refractory requirement
90–200°C

Hot and High-Temperature Water Boilers

A reliable heat source for industrial facilities, residential buildings and district heating systems. Modern design and high efficiency provide long-lasting, economical and environmentally friendly solutions.

  • Hot water: 90–110°C, high-temperature water: 180–200°C
  • Three-pass flame and fire-tube or water-tube design
  • Automatic control systems
  • Compatible with a wide range of fuels
Heat Recovery

Waste Heat Boilers

High-efficiency heat recovery systems that harness the energy of hot gases generated in industrial processes to produce thermal oil, steam or hot/high-temperature water.

  • Energy generation from exhaust and process gases
  • High efficiency, low operating cost
  • Maximum energy recovery with an economizer
  • Automatic control and safety
20–22 bar Steam

Thermal Oil Steam Generators

Compact steam generation systems that use heat transfer oil to provide safe, efficient heating at steam pressures of up to 20–22 bar. They offer faster and easier operation than conventional steam boilers.

  • Operating temperatures up to 300°C
  • Safe operation at low pressure
  • High thermal efficiency and fast start-up
  • Temperature control within ±1°C
  • Compact design
Up to 1000°C

Hot Gas Generators

Purpose-built heat generation systems that make the hot gases produced by burning solid, liquid or gaseous fuels directly usable in industrial processes. They deliver hot gas at temperatures of up to 1000°C.

  • Direct or indirect heat transfer
  • Hot gas generation up to 1000°C
  • Fast commissioning
  • Automatic control
