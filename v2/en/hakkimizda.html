About Us · Our Story

47 years built on the vision of two engineers

Industrial energy solutions since 1979.

BERSEY Ltd. was founded in Istanbul in 1979, when two mechanical engineers came together to establish a modest engineering firm. Over the 47 successful years since, what began with engineering projects and a wide range of industrial process applications has grown into complete turnkey system solutions covering design, manufacturing, installation, and commissioning — both in Turkey and abroad.

Operating primarily in the field of industrial process energy, BERSEY delivers science-based solutions entirely of its own design across many industries, chiefly wood and forest products, paper, chemicals, paint, textiles, vegetable oil, plastics, and packaging.

Engineered Solutions — an expert team, international standards: BERSEY meets its customers' demands and needs with an engineering staff specialized in their fields and a strong technical team. Committed to producing “Engineered Solutions” in line with international norms and standards, as modern technology requires, BERSEY documents its quality of service with CE, ISO, TUV, TSE, EAC (GOST-R), and Gospromnadzor certifications.

Our History

A journey of achievement stretching from 1979 to today

  • 1979 — Founded: Established in Istanbul by two mechanical engineers as a modest engineering firm.
  • 1985 — First Industrial Project: Successfully completed its first large-scale industrial energy project in Turkey.
  • 1995 — Entry into International Markets: Stepped onto the international stage with its first project abroad.
  • 2002 — ISO 9001 Certification: Quality management system certified to ISO 9001.
  • 2007 — ASME U & S Stamp: Obtained ASME U and S Stamp certifications, registering compliance with international standards.
  • 2013 — 100th Project: Delivered its 100th reference project and became a sector leader in biomass energy systems.
  • 2019 — 40th Anniversary & National Board: Celebrated its 40th anniversary. National Board membership brought production to US standards.
  • 2026 — 47 Years, 235+ Projects, 14 Countries: Today, with more than 235 references in 14 countries, Bersey is one of Turkey's leading industrial energy companies.
