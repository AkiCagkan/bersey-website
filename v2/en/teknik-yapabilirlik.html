TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES

Technical Capabilities

Our engineering strength and manufacturing infrastructure

01 · Manufacturing

Production Capacity & Equipment Park

Our modern manufacturing facility is fully equipped for the fabrication of industrial boilers and pressure equipment. Annual production capacity is managed flexibly according to workload.

🏭 Production Area

12,000 m² of covered production space plus a 5,000 m² open storage yard

✂️ CNC Plasma & Laser Cutting

CNC-controlled plasma and oxy-fuel cutting machines, high-precision sheet metal processing

🌀 Rolling & Forming

3-roll and 4-roll hydraulic plate bending machines (up to 80 mm thickness), flange bending presses

⚙️ Turning & Machining Center

CNC lathes, flange drilling and turning, pipe-end machining equipment

🏗️ Cranes & Handling

Overhead bridge cranes with 50-ton capacity, handling & assembly capability up to 200 tons

02 · Welding

Welding Procedures

We manufacture to the highest quality standards with approved welding procedures (WPS/PQR) in accordance with ASME and EN codes. All of our welders hold international certifications.

🏅 ASME Welding Standards

WPS/PQR procedures per ASME Section IX, U & S Stamp certified fabrication

🇪🇺 EN 13480 / EN 12952 / EN 12953

CE-marked fabrication under the European Pressure Equipment Directive (PED)

🔥 Welding Processes

SMAW, GTAW (TIG), GMAW (MIG/MAG), SAW (submerged arc), and FCAW procedures

👷 Certified Welders

A team of 40+ welders certified to ASME Section IX and EN ISO 9606-1

♨️ Heat Treatment (PWHT)

Post-weld heat treatment (PWHT) capability with automatic temperature-controlled furnaces

03 · Testing & Inspection

Testing & Inspection Capabilities

We provide comprehensive quality control and non-destructive testing (NDT) services at every stage of production. All tests are performed in accordance with international standards.

☢️ Radiographic Testing (RT)

Weld seam inspection with X-ray and gamma ray, film evaluation per ASME & EN standards

📡 Ultrasonic Testing (UT)

Weld seam and material thickness measurement, lamination checks

🧲 Magnetic Particle (MT) & Liquid Penetrant (PT)

MT and PT testing for the detection of surface and near-surface defects

💧 Hydrostatic Testing

Test pressure of 1.5 times the design pressure applied to pressure equipment and boiler shells

📏 Dimensional Control & Documentation

Laser measuring instruments, preparation of MDR (Manufacturer Data Report) and quality dossiers

04 · Engineering

Design & Engineering Software

Our experienced engineering team provides project design, strength calculation, and thermal analysis services using industry-standard software.

🖥️ 3D Design & Modeling

3D boiler and equipment design with SolidWorks, AutoCAD, and Inventor

🧮 Strength Calculation

Pressure vessel design to ASME Section VIII Div.1/2, EN 13445, and EN 12952 codes

🌡️ Thermal & Flow Analysis

Boiler thermal performance calculations, flue gas analysis, and combustion optimization

🔧 Pipe Stress Analysis

Piping stress analysis with CAESAR II, thermal expansion and support design

📊 Finite Element Analysis (FEA)

Structural analysis of critical components with ANSYS and SolidWorks Simulation
