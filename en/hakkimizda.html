Our Story

47 years that began with the vision of two engineers

BERSEY Ltd. was founded in Istanbul in 1979 as a modest engineering firm established by two mechanical engineers. Throughout its successful 47-year history, our journey — which began with engineering projects and numerous industrial process applications — continues today both in Turkey and abroad with complete turnkey system solutions covering design, manufacturing, installation and commissioning.

Operating primarily in the field of Industrial Process Energy, BERSEY delivers scientific solutions based entirely on its own designs for a wide range of industrial applications, most notably Wood and Forest Products, Paper, Chemicals, Paint, Textiles, Vegetable Oil, Plastics and Packaging.
