BRV / BRV-RK Series

Thermal Oil Boilers

Bersey offers complete thermal oil boiler and waste heat recovery systems fired by gaseous, liquid or solid fuels. Thermal oil systems are a reliable way of providing continuous heat at high temperatures and operate under low system pressures.

  • BRV Model: Double helical coil design, 465 kW – 23 MW capacity range
  • BRV-RK Model: 12 MW – 46 MW capacity, radiant and convective heat transfer
  • Efficiency of up to 90% (LHV)
  • Low film temperature thanks to double helical coil design
  • Safe operating temperature up to 350°C
Scotch Type

Steam Boilers

Complete steam boiler and waste heat recovery systems fired by gaseous, liquid or solid fuels

BRS Type

3-Pass Scotch Steam Boilers

BRS type Steam Boilers are manufactured in a capacity range of 1,000–20,000 kg/h. They are cylindrical-shell, burner-fired, fire-tube boilers. Thanks to the 3-pass design, flue gases remain inside the boiler longer, providing high heat transfer.

  • Cylindrical shell, resistant to high pressure
  • Fully cylindrical furnace, homogeneous combustion
  • High heat transfer surface with smoke tubes
  • 3-pass design, low flue gas temperature
  • Increased system efficiency with economizer
Membrane Water Wall

Combined Steam Boilers

A solution created by combining a membrane water-wall combustion chamber with single-pass flame and smoke tube boilers. The combustion chamber is surrounded by water-cooled membrane walls.

  • Safe operation with membrane water walls
  • Single-pass flue gas flow, compact design
  • High thermal efficiency, low operating cost
  • Durable combustion chamber with minimized refractory requirement
Water Tube

Water Tube Steam Boilers

Membrane-wall water tube steam boilers are modern and reliable energy generation systems designed for industrial applications requiring high pressure and capacity.

  • Membrane water-wall combustion chamber
  • Water tube design, high pressure and capacity
  • Fast steam generation and high efficiency
  • Minimal refractory requirement
90–200°C

Hot and Superheated Water Boilers

A reliable heat source for industrial facilities, residential buildings and district heating systems. Thanks to modern design and high efficiency, they offer long-lasting, economical and environmentally friendly solutions.

  • Hot water: 90–110°C, superheated water: 180–200°C
  • Three-pass flame and smoke tube or water tube design
  • Automatic control systems
  • Compatible with various fuels
Heat Recovery

Waste Heat Boilers

High-efficiency heat recovery systems that use the energy of hot gases generated in industrial processes to produce thermal oil, steam or hot/superheated water.

  • Energy generation from exhaust and process gases
  • High efficiency, low operating cost
  • Maximum energy recovery with economizer
  • Automatic control and safety
20–22 bar Steam

Thermal Oil Heated Steam Generators

Compact steam generation systems that use heat transfer oil to provide safe and efficient heating at steam pressures of up to 20-22 bar. They offer faster and easier operation compared to conventional steam boilers.

  • Operating temperature up to 300°C
  • Safe operation at low pressure
  • High thermal efficiency and fast start-up
  • Temperature control accuracy of ±1°C
  • Compact design
Up to 1000°C

Hot Gas Generators

Special heat generation systems that make the hot gases obtained from the combustion of solid, liquid or gaseous fuels directly usable in processes. They provide hot gas generation up to 1000°C.

  • Direct or indirect heat transfer
  • Hot gas generation up to 1000°C
  • Fast commissioning
  • Automatic control
