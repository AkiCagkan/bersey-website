02 · Welding

Welding Procedures

With our approved welding procedures (WPS/PQR) compliant with ASME and EN standards, we manufacture to the highest quality standards. All of our welders hold international certifications.

🏅

ASME Welding Standards

WPS/PQR procedures in accordance with ASME Section IX, U & S Stamp certified production

🇪🇺

EN 13480 / EN 12952 / EN 12953

CE-marked production under the European Pressure Equipment Directive (PED)

🔥

Welding Methods

SMAW, GTAW (TIG), GMAW (MIG/MAG), SAW (Submerged Arc), FCAW procedures

👷

Certified Welders

Team of 40+ welders certified to ASME Section IX and EN ISO 9606-1

♨️

Heat Treatment (PWHT)

Post-weld heat treatment (PWHT) capability, furnaces with automatic temperature control

03 · Testing & Inspection

Testing & Inspection Facilities

We provide comprehensive quality control and non-destructive testing (NDT) services at every stage of the production process. All tests are performed in accordance with international standards.

☢️

Radiographic Testing (RT)

Weld seam inspection with X-Ray and Gamma rays, film evaluation in accordance with ASME & EN standards

📡

Ultrasonic Testing (UT)

Weld seam and material thickness measurement, lamination inspection

🧲

Magnetic Particle (MT) & Liquid Penetrant (PT)

MT and PT testing for the detection of surface and subsurface defects

💧

Hydrostatic Testing

Test pressure of 1.5 times the design pressure applied to pressure equipment and boiler shells

📏

Dimensional Control & Documentation

Laser measurement devices, preparation of MDR (Manufacturer Data Report) and quality dossiers

04 · Engineering

Design & Engineering Software

🖥️

3D Design & Modeling

3D boiler and equipment design with SolidWorks, AutoCAD and Inventor

🧮

Strength Calculation

Thermal & Flow Analysis
