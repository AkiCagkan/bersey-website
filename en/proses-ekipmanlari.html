Deaerators

Deaerators

In steam systems, our deaerators prevent corrosion by removing oxygen and other gases from the feed water, extending the service life of your boilers and installations.

  • Removal of oxygen and dissolved gases
  • Full compatibility with steam boilers
  • Automatic control systems
  • Corrosion prevention and extended boiler life
  • Low operating cost
Water Softening

Water Softening Units

For industrial steam and hot water systems, they remove water hardness to prevent limescale, reduce energy losses and protect your systems.

  • Hardness removal technology
  • Automatic regeneration
  • Suitable for steam and hot water systems
  • PLC-controlled operation
  • Limescale prevention and extended system life
Flue Gas Cleaning

Flue Gas Cleaning Systems

  • Full compliance with environmental standards
