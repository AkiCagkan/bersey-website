Four core combustion technologies

High-efficiency, low-emission combustion solutions for solid, dust, liquid and gaseous fuels. Each system is engineered according to the fuel type and capacity requirements.

Grate Firing

Grate Combustion Systems

A widely used combustion technology in which solid fuels are burned in a controlled manner. Fuel is fed onto the grate surface, and complete combustion is achieved by supplying controlled air from below and/or above.

  • Stoker-fed, fixed, moving and chain grate types
  • Ability to burn medium and low-grade fuels
  • Controlled combustion with zoned air distribution
  • High mechanical strength and long service life
  • Wide fuel flexibility: coal, biomass, RDF
  • Low investment and operating costs
  • Adaptable to different capacities
Fluidized Bed

Fluidized Bed Combustion Systems

A modern combustion technology that enables solid fuels to be burned with high efficiency and low emissions. The sand/inert material at the bottom of the furnace is fluidized with pressurized air, and the fuel burns homogeneously in suspension.

  • 90%+ combustion efficiency, low emissions
  • Wide fuel flexibility (coal, biomass, RDF)
  • Controlled combustion at 800-900°C, low NOx
  • SO₂ reduction with limestone addition
  • Homogeneous temperature distribution
  • Low ash and slag formation
  • Durable and reliable system
Dust & Granule Firing

Dust and Granule Combustion Systems

A special combustion technology that enables coal dust, wood dust, biomass granules and industrial combustible dusts to be burned with high efficiency. The fuel is ground and fed via pneumatic conveying and special burners.

  • Design suited to dust and granule fuels
  • High combustion efficiency and low emissions
  • Automatic fuel feeding and control
  • Low ash and slag formation
  • Modular design, wide capacity range
  • Utilization of various biomass and waste streams
  • Low operating costs
Gas & Liquid Fuel

Gas & Liquid Fuel Combustion Systems (Burner Systems)

Industrial burner systems that enable natural gas, LPG, fuel oil, diesel and other liquid/gaseous fuels to be burned with high efficiency. With automatic control, low emissions and high combustion efficiency, they are a core component of energy generation plants.

  • Natural gas, LPG, fuel oil and dual-fuel options
  • Fully automatic ignition and modulating control
  • High combustion efficiency (95%+)
  • Low NOx and CO emissions
  • Wide capacity range (100 kW – 50 MW)
  • Instant combustion start-up and shutdown
  • Precise temperature and pressure control
