Engineering & Consulting

Engineering Services

Pipe routing, pipe stress analysis, thermal & strength calculations, equipment design, and end-to-end engineering consulting.

Piping & Pipe Stress Analysis

We determine the safest, most economical, and most efficient routes for the piping serving a plant's process and auxiliary systems, model them in a 3D environment, and evaluate the stresses that may arise in the lines through engineering calculations.

Scope of services:

  • 3D pipe routing design and modeling (with CAD/CAE software)
  • Line planning coordinated with equipment layouts
  • Stress calculations to ASME, EN, ANSI, and API standards
  • Analysis of thermal, static, and dynamic loads
  • Computer-aided modeling with ROHR2 and similar software
  • Support design and optimization for critical points
  • Preparation of result reports and project documentation

Thermal & Strength Calculations

Calculation and verification of the thermal performance and mechanical strength of industrial equipment in accordance with international standards.

Scope of services:

  • Thermal calculations for boilers, heat exchangers, and pressure vessels
  • Strength calculations to ASME, EN, and AD 2000 standards
  • Stress and deformation simulations with finite element analysis (FEA)
  • Thermal expansion and fatigue analyses
  • Material selection and thickness optimization
  • Preparation of calculation reports and approval processes

Process Equipment Design & Consulting

Design of the pressure vessels, tanks, heat exchangers, deaerators, filters, and auxiliary equipment used in industrial plants in accordance with international standards, together with end-to-end engineering consulting.

Scope of services:

  • Technical calculations tailored to process requirements (pressure, temperature, flow rate)
  • Compliance with ASME, EN, PED, and AD 2000 standards
  • 3D modeling and detailed fabrication drawings
  • Consulting on boiler selection, capacity sizing, and fuel analysis
  • Consulting on emission control and efficiency optimization
  • Technical support through project management, installation, and commissioning

After-Sales Services & Spare Parts

We provide comprehensive after-sales support, maintenance services, and genuine spare parts for every system we deliver.

Scope of services:

  • Periodic maintenance and inspection services
  • Troubleshooting, repair, and overhaul work
  • Genuine spare parts supply and logistics support
  • Remote monitoring and technical support line
  • Capacity upgrade and modernization projects
  • Training and certification programs for operating personnel

Our experienced engineering team stands by you at every stage of your project.
