About Us · Our Story

47 years built on the vision of two engineers

Industrial energy solutions since 1979.

Two mechanical engineers came together in Istanbul in 1979 and founded BERSEY Ltd. as a modest engineering firm. The journey that began with engineering projects and a wide range of industrial process applications has carried us through 47 years; today we deliver complete turnkey system solutions — design, manufacturing, installation, and commissioning — both in Turkey and abroad.

Our core business is industrial process energy. We build science-based solutions entirely of our own design for many industries — chiefly wood and forest products, paper, chemicals, paint, textiles, vegetable oil, plastics, and packaging.

Engineered Solutions — an expert team, international standards: We answer our customers' demands and needs with engineers who are specialists in their fields and a strong technical team. Producing “Engineered Solutions” in line with the international norms and standards modern technology requires is a principle we hold ourselves to — and we back our quality of service with CE, ISO, TUV, TSE, EAC (GOST-R), and Gospromnadzor certifications.

Our History

A journey of achievement stretching from 1979 to today

  • 1979 — Founded: We started out in Istanbul: two mechanical engineers and a modest engineering firm.
  • 1985 — First Industrial Project: We successfully completed our first large-scale industrial energy project in Turkey.
  • 1995 — Entry into International Markets: Our first project abroad took us into international markets.
  • 2002 — ISO 9001 Certification: We had our quality management system certified to ISO 9001.
  • 2007 — ASME U & S Stamp: We obtained the ASME U and S Stamp certifications, registering our compliance with international standards.
  • 2013 — 100th Project: We delivered our 100th reference project and took our place among the leading companies in biomass energy systems.
  • 2019 — 40th Anniversary & National Board: We celebrated our 40th anniversary; National Board membership brought our production to US standards.
  • 2026 — 47 Years, 235+ Projects, 14 Countries: Today, with more than 235 references in 14 countries, we stand among Turkey's leading industrial energy companies.
